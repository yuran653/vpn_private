Personal VPS/Thailand

One private road from your phone to the internet, by way of Bangkok

A VLESS server on an Ubuntu VPS, reached only through Cloudflare. To anyone watching the wire it looks like HTTPS to a small file‑sync site behind Cloudflare, one of millions. Your traffic leaves through Cloudflare WARP in Thailand, and the server closes every leak it can control: DNS, IPv6, fingerprinting and panel discovery.

02Step by step

What happens to one request

1 · Device

An HTTPS visit to a Cloudflare site

Happ looks up files.casedigestai.com, gets a Cloudflare address, and opens TLS to it with an Android client's fingerprint, offering HTTP/1.1 only (ADR-0015). Your traffic travels as VLESS in the bodies of ordinary HTTP requests (XHTTP), inside that HTTPS. The VPS address never appears on your network.

2 · Shape

Short connections, padded requests

The link tells the client to stop putting new sessions on a connection after 2 to 5 minutes or 32 to 64 sessions, and to pad every request with 100 to 1000 random bytes. The server pads its responses the same way. Long use looks like many short browser connections, not one that lasts an hour (ADR‑0014).

3 · Cloudflare

Front door

Cloudflare ends your TLS at its site nearest to you, then opens its own TLS to the VPS and checks the server's Let's Encrypt certificate ("Full (strict)"). The VPS firewall admits ports 80 and 443 only from Cloudflare's published ranges (ADR‑0013).

4 · nginx

One path or the cover site

nginx holds port 443. Requests for one random path, kept on the server and in your link, go to the Xray inbound on loopback. Every other path gets the CaseDigest AI site from a Docker container on loopback (ADR‑0012).

5 · Routing

Three rules

  • Port 53 to any address → answered by the server's own DNS
  • Any IPv6 destination → blocked
  • Everything else → warp, the only outbound
6 · Exit

Out through Bangkok

Xray runs WireGuard to WARP in user space, IPv4 only, MTU 1280. Cloudflare's Bangkok site is 1.4 ms away. Sites see a WARP address placed in Thailand, so YouTube Music works.

03Location

Why Google's AI says you are in Bangkok

Gemini and other Google services guess where you are from the IP address that reaches them. Through this VPN, that address always belongs to Cloudflare WARP in Thailand.

  1. Google sees one address, and it is not yours

    Every request leaves the VPS through the warp outbound, and there is no other way out. DNS goes the same way, and IPv6 is blocked. So Google sees neither your home address nor the VPS address, only the WARP address.

  2. That address is Cloudflare's, in Thailand

    The WireGuard tunnel enters Cloudflare at its Bangkok site, 1.4 ms from the VPS. Cloudflare places the exit address in Thailand (loc=TH). Geo‑IP databases, Google's included, put that address in Thailand at city level, which means Bangkok. That is the location Gemini shows as "based on your IP address".

  3. The VPS's own address would say Myanmar

    Google places the server's own address in Myanmar. Before WARP, YouTube Music said "not available in this country". Because the server has no direct outbound, Google never sees that address, even when WARP is down. Requests fail instead.

Cloudflare's report, through the tunnel
warp=on # R-vpn-server-12.a loc=TH # R-vpn-server-12.b colo=BKK # R-vpn-server-12.f

scripts/verify-vpn.sh fetches /cdn-cgi/trace through the tunnel and fails if any of these lines changes. It also checks that YouTube Music opens.

What the server cannot control

Google can also use signals from your device and account. If the phone shares precise location with Google, or your account's home country or the browser's time zone say otherwise, Google can show a different place. The VPN only decides which IP address Google sees.

05Leak guards

Each way you could be exposed, and what closes it

DNS leak

Every lookup answered inside

A query to port 53 of any address, even one with no resolver, is caught and answered by the server. The server asks over DNS‑over‑HTTPS (1.1.1.1, 8.8.8.8) through WARP.

R‑vpn‑server‑03
IPv6 egress

IPv6 does not exist here

Turned off in the kernel with ipv6.disable=1 from the first moment of boot. IPv6 destinations are blocked and AAAA lookups come back empty.

R‑vpn‑server‑02 · ADR‑0004
DPI fingerprinting

Ordinary HTTPS to Cloudflare

Your ISP sees an ordinary TLS hello to a Cloudflare address, the same addresses that serve millions of sites. Inside, the tunnel is plain HTTP requests, rotated across short connections and padded, so neither a hosting address nor one hour‑long connection gives it away.

R‑vpn‑server‑17 · 18 · ADR‑0013 · ADR‑0014
Panel and server discovery

Nothing to find

3x‑ui and the Xray inbound listen on 127.0.0.1; the panel is reached through ssh -L. Ports 80 and 443 answer only Cloudflare, so the VPS address shows no site and no tunnel. Through Cloudflare, every path but one is the cover site.

R‑vpn‑server‑04 · 05 · ADR‑0002
Exit through the VPS address

Fails closed

There is no direct outbound. If WARP is down, requests fail. They never fall back to the server's address, which Google places in Myanmar.

R‑vpn‑server‑12 · ADR‑0008
Activity record

Nothing to hand over

Xray's access log and DNS log are off, and neither nginx nor the site container writes an access log. Keys, tokens and the client link sit in root‑only files and never appear in script output.

06The host

A small, closed, self‑patching box

  • Firewallufw denies all incoming except SSH from anywhere, and 80/tcp and 443/tcp from Cloudflare's published IPv4 ranges, refreshed on each deploy.
  • Flood limitsSSH: 10 open or 30 new per minute per address. Port 443 has no per‑address limit, because every client arrives from a shared Cloudflare address; Cloudflare absorbs floods first. SYN cookies on, backlog 4096.
  • SSHKeys only, no root, no passwords. Unauthenticated connections drop after 30 s. fail2ban watches the rest.
  • Webnginx with a Let's Encrypt certificate for casedigestai.com, files. and status., renewed by certbot. The site runs in Docker on 127.0.0.1.
  • Updatesunattended‑upgrades installs security fixes and reboots at 01:00 Bangkok time when one needs it.
  • SpeedBBR with fq pacing, 16 MB socket buffers, no slow start after idle.
